02 · Voice agent

It calls you. Twice a day.

A real phone call — your calendar, your tasks, your habits, your projects. The agent talks, you talk back, things actually change in your workspace.

7:30 AM · Wednesday
Good morning, Saumya. You have a 2 pm call with Maya — want me to move gym to 4?
Yes, move it to 4.
Done. Three high-priority tasks today: ship voice agent v2, review Aurora docs, and book flights.
Mark "book flights" as done.
Marked. Have a calm one.

Powered by Groq llama-3.3-70b with tool calling.

During a call the agent has direct, authenticated access to your data — and the tools to actually change things, not just talk about them.

complete_task reschedule_task delete_task create_task set_task_time change_priority complete_habit skip_habit end_conversation
  • 📅 Pulls today's calendar events — including Google-synced ones — to suggest free slots.
  • 📊 Evening calls weave in a short project-progress recap: "Aurora is 70% done, 2 tasks left this week."
  • 🧠 Persistent long-term memory across sessions — the agent remembers what you told it yesterday.
  • 🌍 Runs at your local time, in your timezone — not some UTC default.

04 · Architecture

How the pieces talk.

A single Next.js app handles every surface — web, API routes, and Twilio webhooks. Supabase holds state; Groq, Gemini, Twilio, and Resend are leaf services. Background work fans out from Supabase pg_cron, not Vercel.

User ⇄ App App ⇄ Database pg_cron hourly fan-out App ⇄ AI / Comms Phone ⇄ Twilio voice loop
05 · Daily rituals

A day, choreographed.

Every hour, Supabase pg_cron pings the app. The app checks each user's local time and fans out exactly the rituals that match. No Vercel Pro plan required.

07:30 local · morning

Voice call · plan the day

Twilio dials you. The agent reads today's calendar, walks through your task list, and reschedules whatever doesn't fit. POST /api/voice/outbound

08:00 local · morning

Digest · email + WhatsApp

Resend sends your morning summary; Twilio mirrors it to WhatsApp if enabled. POST /api/notifications/morning-digest

19:00 local · evening

Reflective call · close the day

What you finished. What's left. A short project-progress recap. The agent listens, then logs everything to voice_sessions.

Sunday 04:00 UTC

Weekly retrospective

An auto-generated weekly summary email — completion rate, habit streaks, and a Gemini-written insight paragraph. POST /api/retrospective
